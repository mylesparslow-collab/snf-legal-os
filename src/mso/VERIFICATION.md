## Where the research report differs

- AG injunctive relief and fees are § 1191(e), not § 1192. § 1192 is severability.
- The report's "§ 1191(c) regulatory-reporting carve-out" is § 1191(d)(3)(B).
- "Clinical staffing ratios" is not in § 1191. The text covers patient counts and hours ((a)(1)(D)) and hiring or firing based on clinical competency ((a)(2)(B)).
- SB 351 covers physician and dental practices only. It does not reach NP practices or skilled nursing facilities.
- § 13401.5 has a headcount limit as well as the 49% share limit. The report gives only the 49%.
- A nursing corporation is not "100% RN/NP"; § 13401.5(f) lets physicians and other listed licensees hold up to 49%.
- The 104 NP path has been in effect since Jan. 1, 2023. The report presents it as a Jan. 1, 2026 regime.
- § 650(b) does not say "fair market value"; it says commensurate with the value of the services.
- The report's "non-§ 16601" wording: § 1191(d)(3)(A) says "an otherwise enforceable sale of business noncompete agreement" and does not cite § 16601.

## Not checked

- Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 16600 and 16601 (employee noncompetes, sale of business). The Okafor clause is sent there for separate review.
- Bus. & Prof. Code § 2400 and the corporate practice of medicine case law (kept in place by § 1191(g)).
- The constitutional effective-date rule (SB 351 has no urgency clause, so Jan. 1, 2026 is assumed) and whether SB 351 reaches contracts signed before then.
- License status. The license numbers are fictional; nothing was looked up in DCA BreEZe.
