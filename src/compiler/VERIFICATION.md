## Readings an attorney should confirm

These are marked "Our reading" in the app and the exports:

- Treating LLC members like corporate shareholders under § 1267.5, which speaks of corporations and partnerships.
- Listing a 0% special member with consent rights as a prospective owner for CDPH.
- Applying the CMS organizational-structure rules to every LLC and LP in the chain, not only the enrolling SNF.
- Treating an interest in the property-owning company as an interest in the real property for the CMS 5% test.
- Moving a "file before" deadline that lands on a weekend or holiday to the business day before it.
